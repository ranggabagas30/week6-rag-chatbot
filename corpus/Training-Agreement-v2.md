# **1\. The Program**

1.  The Trainer will deliver the “AI Engineer Career Pivot Program” (the “Program”), a 24-week part-time training program delivered on weekends, as described in the syllabus attached as Schedule A (the “Syllabus”).
2.  The Program comprises: live weekend sessions (approximately 4 hours on Saturdays and 3 hours of guided lab/office hours on Sundays), weekly homework, three milestone projects, one capstone project, mock interviews, and a final demo day.
3.  Program dates: from \[START DATE\] to \[END DATE\]. The Trainer may adjust individual session dates with at least 7 days’ written notice, provided the total contact hours are preserved.
4.  The Trainer may update the Syllabus content to keep it current with industry tooling, provided the learning outcomes remain substantially equivalent.

# **2\. Fees and Payment**

1.  The total Program fee is IDR 9.800.000 (the “Fee”), inclusive/exclusive of GST as stated in the invoice.
2.  Payment schedule: full payment on signing.
3.  The Trainee is responsible for their own equipment and third-party costs, including a laptop capable of running Docker and API usage costs estimated at USD 30–50 per month.
4.  Fees not received within 14 days of the due date may result in suspension of access to sessions and materials until payment is made.

# **3\. Trainer’s Obligations**

1.  Deliver the live sessions, labs, and office hours described in the Syllabus.
2.  Provide course materials, starter repositories, slide decks, and written guides for each week of the Program.
3.  Review and give feedback on submitted homework, milestone projects, and the capstone within a reasonable time (target: before the following weekend’s session).
4.  Conduct at least two mock interviews per Trainee and provide written feedback.
5.  Organise a demo day and provide career support activities described in Month 6 of the Syllabus.
6.  Provide a completion certificate to Trainees who meet the completion requirements in Clause 5.

# **4\. Trainee’s Obligations**

1.  Attend live sessions and participate in labs, committing approximately 15–17 hours per week including self-paced work.
2.  Submit weekly homework, the three milestone projects, and the capstone by their respective deadlines.
3.  Conduct themselves professionally toward the Trainer and other cohort members; harassment or disruptive behaviour may result in termination under Clause 12.
4.  Not share, resell, or redistribute course materials (see Clause 8).
5.  Keep login credentials for cohort platforms confidential and not share access with third parties.

# **5\. Job/Project Guarantee and Refund**

1.  Subject to this Clause 5, if the Trainee does not obtain a Qualifying Outcome within twelve (12) months after the Program end date, the Trainer will refund 80% of the Fee actually paid.
2.  A “Qualifying Outcome” means either: (a) an offer of employment (full-time, part-time, or contract) in an AI engineering or substantially related technical role; or (b) one or more paid projects or freelance engagements in AI engineering with an aggregate value of at least IDR 7.500.000 in each case within the Location Scope defined in Clause 5.3.
3.  "Location Scope" means a role or engagement that is fully remote and performable from the Trainee's home location stated in Schedule B. Any offer that is not fully remote — including any on-site or hybrid arrangement — is outside the Location Scope, and declining such an offer does not affect the guarantee or count against the Trainee.
4.  The guarantee applies only if the Trainee has met ALL of the following eligibility conditions (the “Eligibility Conditions”):
    1.  attended at least 90% of live sessions — for the standard schedule of 48 weekend sessions (24 Saturdays + 24 Sundays), this means no more than 4 missed sessions; a session made up through a rescheduled or catch-up arrangement approved by the Trainer counts as attended;
    2.  submitted all weekly homework and all three milestone projects by their deadlines (or approved extended deadlines);
    3.  completed and deployed the capstone project to its written acceptance criteria;
    4.  within 90 days after the Program end date, applied to at least 40 relevant roles and/or submitted at least 10 freelance proposals, and retained evidence of each (e.g., application confirmations, sent proposals);
    5.  attended all scheduled mock interviews and made reasonable efforts to implement feedback; and
    6.  participated in the monthly completion checklist reviews with the Trainer.
5.  The guarantee does not apply if the Trainee: declines a bona fide offer that constitutes a Qualifying Outcome within the Location Scope; restricts their job or project search more narrowly than the parameters agreed in Schedule B without the Trainer’s written agreement; provides false information in applications; or becomes unavailable for work during the guarantee window. For the avoidance of doubt, declining an offer outside the Location Scope (e.g., a non-remote role requiring relocation) does not void the guarantee.
6.  The refund under this Clause 5 is the Trainee’s sole and exclusive remedy in respect of the guarantee.

# **6\. Refund Claim Process**

1.  To claim, the Trainee must give written notice to the Trainer within 30 days after the end of the twelve-month guarantee window, enclosing the evidence described in Clause 5.4(d).
2.  The Trainer will assess the claim and respond in writing within 21 days. Approved refunds will be paid within 30 days of approval.
3.  If the Parties disagree on eligibility, the dispute process in Clause 13 applies.

# **7\. Attendance, Deferral and Make-Up**

1.  Recordings or catch-up materials for missed sessions may be provided at the Trainer’s discretion. The Trainer will offer reasonable rescheduling or make-up arrangements where requested in advance; a completed make-up counts as attendance for the purposes of Clause 5.
2.  The Trainee may defer once to the next available cohort for medical or compassionate reasons, evidenced in writing, subject to a deferral fee of IDR 1.000.000 (waivable at the Trainer’s discretion).

1.  All course materials (slides, guides, starter code, recordings, curriculum) are and remain the property of the Trainer. The Trainee receives a personal, non-transferable, non-commercial licence to use them for their own learning.
2.  Code and projects written by the Trainee during the Program (including the capstone) belong to the Trainee.
3.  The Trainee grants the Trainer a non-exclusive licence to reference the Trainee’s projects (with attribution) for teaching and marketing, unless the Trainee opts out in writing.

# **9\. Confidentiality and Personal Data (PDPA)**

1.  Each Party will keep confidential any non-public information of the other disclosed during the Program.
2.  The Trainer will collect, use, and disclose the Trainee’s personal data only for delivering the Program, administering the guarantee, and (with consent) marketing, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore).
3.  The Trainee may withdraw consent or request access/correction by writing to \[CONTACT EMAIL\]. Withdrawal of consent essential to Program delivery may require termination under Clause 12.

# **10\. Media and Demo Day**

1.  Demo day and selected sessions may be recorded or photographed. The Trainee consents to reasonable use of such media for Program archives and marketing. The Trainee may opt out of marketing use by written notice.

# **11\. Disclaimers and Limitation of Liability**

1.  Except for the guarantee in Clause 5, the Trainer does not warrant that the Trainee will obtain any particular role, employer, salary, or project.
2.  Career outcomes depend substantially on the Trainee’s own effort, background, and market conditions.
3.  To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Trainer’s total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the Fee actually paid by the Trainee. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

# **12\. Termination and Withdrawal**

1.  Cooling-off: the Trainee may withdraw within 7 days of signing (and before attending the second session) for a full refund less any non-recoverable costs.
2.  After the cooling-off period, if the Trainee withdraws: fees for completed months are non-refundable; fees for future months are refundable on a pro-rated basis, less a 10% of administrative fee.
3.  The Trainer may terminate for the Trainee’s material breach (including non-payment or misconduct) not remedied within 14 days of written notice. In that case the guarantee in Clause 5 lapses.
4.  If the Trainer cancels the Program or is unable to deliver it substantially as described, the Trainee receives a pro-rated refund of the undelivered portion.

1.  This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
2.  The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith negotiation, then by mediation at the Singapore Mediation Centre, before commencing proceedings in the courts of Singapore.

2.  Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
3.  Notices may be given by email to the addresses stated above or subsequently notified in writing.

## **1\. The Program**

1.1 The Trainer will deliver the "AI Engineer Career Pivot Program" (the "Program"), a 24-week part-time training program delivered on weekends, as described in the syllabus attached as Schedule A (the "Syllabus").

1.2 The Program comprises: live weekend sessions (approximately 4 hours on Saturdays and 3 hours of guided lab/office hours on Sundays), weekly homework, three milestone projects, one capstone project, mock interviews, and a final demo day.

1.3 Program dates: from \[START DATE\] to \[END DATE\]. The Trainer may adjust individual session dates with at least 7 days' written notice, provided the total contact hours are preserved.

1.4 The Trainer may update the Syllabus content to keep it current with industry tooling, provided the learning outcomes remain substantially equivalent.

## **2\. Fees and Payment**

2.1 The total Program fee is IDR 9.800.000 (the "Fee"), inclusive/exclusive of GST as stated in the invoice.

2.2 The Fee shall be paid in six (6) tranches as set out in Schedule C, each tied to a specific point of delivered progress in the Syllabus rather than to the passage of time alone. Tranche 1 is a seat-securing deposit due on signing. Tranches 2–6 become due only once the Trainer has confirmed in writing that the corresponding milestone in Schedule C has been delivered and meets its stated acceptance criteria.

2.3 The Trainer shall confirm acceptance of, or request revisions to, each milestone within seven (7) days of submission, consistent with Clause 3.3. If the Trainer does not respond within fourteen (14) days of submission, the milestone is deemed accepted for the sole purpose of triggering the next Tranche.

2.4 The Trainee may discontinue the Program at the end of any Tranche period, for any reason, on written notice to the Trainer. On discontinuation: no further Tranches are payable; amounts already paid are non-refundable except as provided in Clause 12; and the Trainee retains access to all materials and recordings for Tranche periods already paid.

2.5 The Trainee is responsible for their own equipment and third-party costs, including a laptop capable of running Docker and API usage costs estimated at USD 30–50 per month.

2.6 A Tranche not received within 14 days of its due date may result in suspension of access to sessions and materials until paid. A Tranche left unpaid for 30 days is treated as a discontinuation notice under Clause 2.4.

2.7 For the avoidance of doubt, the Job/Project Guarantee under Clause 5 applies only where the Trainee completes the Program in full under Clause 5.6 and has paid all six Tranches under Schedule C.

## **3\. Trainer's Obligations**

3.1 Deliver the live sessions, labs, and office hours described in the Syllabus.

3.2 Provide course materials, starter repositories, slide decks, and written guides for each week of the Program.

3.3 Review and give feedback on submitted homework, milestone projects, and the capstone within a reasonable time (target: before the following weekend's session), and in accordance with the confirmation timeline in Clause 2.3.

3.4 Conduct at least two mock interviews per Trainee and provide written feedback.

3.5 Organise a demo day and provide career support activities described in Month 6 of the Syllabus.

3.6 Provide a completion certificate to Trainees who meet the completion requirements in Clause 5.

## **4\. Trainee's Obligations**

4.1 Attend live sessions and participate in labs, committing approximately 15–17 hours per week including self-paced work.

4.2 Submit weekly homework, the three milestone projects, and the capstone by their respective deadlines.

4.3 Conduct themselves professionally toward the Trainer and other cohort members; harassment or disruptive behaviour may result in termination under Clause 12.

4.4 Not share, resell, or redistribute course materials (see Clause 8).

4.5 Keep login credentials for cohort platforms confidential and not share access with third parties.

## **5\. Job/Project Guarantee and Refund**

5.1 Subject to this Clause 5, if the Trainee does not obtain a Qualifying Outcome within twelve (12) months after the Program end date, the Trainer will refund 80% of the Fee actually paid. For the avoidance of doubt, this twelve (12) month guarantee window governs; any reference to a six (6) month window in the Syllabus (Schedule A) or other Program materials is superseded by this Agreement.

5.2 A "Qualifying Outcome" means an outcome satisfying Track A or Track B, as defined in Clauses 5.3 and 5.4.

5.3 Track A — Remote/Freelance Engagement Linked to Singapore. An outcome under Track A is an offer of employment (full-time, part-time, or contract), or one or more freelance/consulting engagements, in an AI engineering or substantially related technical role, where:

(a) the engaging entity is a company registered in Singapore, or a client whose engagement is billed in SGD or invoiced to a Singapore entity;

(b) the role or engagement is performable remotely from the Trainee's home location stated in Schedule B, without relocation or a Singapore work pass; and

(c) the gross compensation is at least IDR 32.000.000 (or its SGD equivalent at the exchange rate on the date of the relevant invoice or payslip) per calendar month, sustained for at least two (2) consecutive months; for freelance engagements without a fixed monthly structure, an aggregate of at least IDR 64.000.000 across any consecutive two-month window satisfies this condition instead.

5.4 Track B — On-Site Singapore Employment. An outcome under Track B is an offer of full-time employment based in Singapore, requiring relocation and a Singapore work pass, where the offered gross salary meets or exceeds the Ministry of Manpower's minimum qualifying salary for an Employment Pass in effect at the date of the offer. As this floor is set by Singapore law and adjusts over time, no fixed IDR figure is set for Track B in this Agreement — the statutory threshold in effect at the time applies automatically.

5.5 "Location Scope" means an engagement or role satisfying Track A or Track B. A role or engagement located in, or requiring relocation to, any country or territory other than Indonesia (Track A) or Singapore (Track B) is outside the Location Scope, and declining such an offer does not affect the guarantee or count against the Trainee.

5.6 The guarantee applies only if the Trainee has met ALL of the following eligibility conditions (the "Eligibility Conditions"):

(a) attended at least 90% of live sessions — for the standard schedule of 48 weekend sessions (24 Saturdays + 24 Sundays), this means no more than 4 missed sessions; a session made up through a rescheduled or catch-up arrangement approved by the Trainer counts as attended;

(b) submitted all weekly homework and all three milestone projects by their deadlines (or approved extended deadlines);

(c) completed and deployed the capstone project to its written acceptance criteria;

(d) within 90 days after the Program end date, applied to at least 40 relevant roles and/or submitted at least 10 freelance proposals, and retained evidence of each (e.g., application confirmations, sent proposals);

(e) attended all scheduled mock interviews and made reasonable efforts to implement feedback; and

(f) participated in the monthly completion checklist reviews with the Trainer.

5.7 The guarantee does not apply if the Trainee:

(a) declines a bona fide offer that constitutes a Qualifying Outcome within the Location Scope (Clause 5.5);

(b) declines any other bona fide offer of employment (full-time, part-time, or contract), or freelance/consulting engagement, in an AI engineering or substantially related technical role, from any employer or client in any country, where the gross compensation offered is at least IDR 32.000.000 (or its equivalent in the relevant currency at the exchange rate on the date of the offer) per calendar month, sustained or reasonably expected to be sustained for at least two (2) consecutive months;

(c) restricts their job or project search more narrowly than the parameters agreed in Schedule B without the Trainer's written agreement;

(d) provides false information in applications; or

(e) becomes unavailable for work during the guarantee window.

For the avoidance of doubt, declining an offer that does not meet the compensation threshold in Clause 5.7(b) — regardless of the country it is offered from — does not affect the guarantee.

5.8 The refund under this Clause 5 is the Trainee's sole and exclusive remedy in respect of the guarantee.

## **6\. Refund Claim Process**

6.1 To claim, the Trainee must give written notice to the Trainer within 30 days after the end of the twelve-month guarantee window, enclosing the evidence described in Clause 5.6(d).

6.2 The Trainer will assess the claim and respond in writing within 21 days. Approved refunds will be paid within 30 days of approval.

6.3 If the Parties disagree on eligibility, the dispute process in Clause 13 applies.

## **7\. Attendance, Deferral and Make-Up**

7.1 Recordings or catch-up materials for missed sessions may be provided at the Trainer's discretion. The Trainer will offer reasonable rescheduling or make-up arrangements where requested in advance; a completed make-up counts as attendance for the purposes of Clause 5.

7.2 The Trainee may defer once to the next available cohort for medical or compassionate reasons, evidenced in writing, subject to a deferral fee of IDR 1.000.000 (waivable at the Trainer's discretion).

## **8\. Intellectual Property**

8.1 All course materials (slides, guides, starter code, recordings, curriculum) are and remain the property of the Trainer. The Trainee receives a personal, non-transferable, non-commercial licence to use them for their own learning.

8.2 Code and projects written by the Trainee during the Program (including the capstone) belong to the Trainee.

8.3 The Trainee grants the Trainer a non-exclusive licence to reference the Trainee's projects (with attribution) for teaching and marketing, unless the Trainee opts out in writing.

## **9\. Confidentiality and Personal Data (PDPA)**

9.1 Each Party will keep confidential any non-public information of the other disclosed during the Program.

9.2 The Trainer will collect, use, and disclose the Trainee's personal data only for delivering the Program, administering the guarantee, and (with consent) marketing, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore).

9.3 The Trainee may withdraw consent or request access/correction by writing to \[CONTACT EMAIL\]. Withdrawal of consent essential to Program delivery may require termination under Clause 12.

## **10\. Media and Demo Day**

10.1 Demo day and selected sessions may be recorded or photographed. The Trainee consents to reasonable use of such media for Program archives and marketing. The Trainee may opt out of marketing use by written notice.

## **11\. Disclaimers and Limitation of Liability**

11.1 Except for the guarantee in Clause 5, the Trainer does not warrant that the Trainee will obtain any particular role, employer, salary, or project.

11.2 Career outcomes depend substantially on the Trainee's own effort, background, and market conditions.

11.3 To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Trainer's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the Fee actually paid by the Trainee. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## **12\. Termination and Withdrawal**

12.1 Cooling-off: the Trainee may withdraw within 7 days of signing (and before attending the second session) for a full refund less any non-recoverable costs.

12.2 After the cooling-off period, discontinuation is governed by Clause 2.4: amounts already paid under completed Tranches are non-refundable, and no further Tranches are payable.

12.3 The Trainer may terminate for the Trainee's material breach (including non-payment or misconduct) not remedied within 14 days of written notice. In that case the guarantee in Clause 5 lapses.

12.4 If the Trainer cancels the Program or is unable to deliver it substantially as described, the Trainee receives a refund of the portion of the most recently paid Tranche corresponding to sessions not yet delivered, calculated on a pro-rata weekly basis; no further Tranches are payable.

## **13\. Governing Law and Disputes**

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.

13.2 The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith negotiation, then by mediation at the Singapore Mediation Centre, before commencing proceedings in the courts of Singapore.

## **14\. General**

14.1 This Agreement (with Schedules A, B and C) is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Program and supersedes prior discussions.

14.2 Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

14.3 Notices may be given by email to the addresses stated above or subsequently notified in writing.

14.4 If any provision is found invalid, the remainder continues in force.
